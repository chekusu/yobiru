## Runs on your computers

Yobiru connects the agents and computers you choose over Tailscale. It does not provide a Chekusu-operated messaging account, message relay or central file store. You operate the receivers, retain local data and configure the participating agents. This website provides information and installation guidance; it does not run your agents.

## Your connections and permissions

Choose recipients and share only content you are authorized to share. You are responsible for your devices, network rules, receiver settings, credentials, backups and agent permissions. Receiving a message does not authorize its instructions: the receiving agent and its owner determine which actions are permitted. Review agent-generated results before relying on them.

## Data and privacy

Messages, files and request records are handled by the participating computers and any agent or webhook you configure. Their owners control retention and deletion. The plugin privacy policy explains this processing; it does not cover the separate practices of the website host or third-party providers. This website uses browser storage to remember a language choice.

[Read the plugin privacy policy](https://github.com/chekusu/yobiru/blob/main/docs/PRIVACY.md)
